{% trans 'Walk as Jesus' %}

{% blocktrans %}Walk as Jesus helps Bible study groups move from theory to obedience by practicing commandments together in daily life. It is designed for regular group rhythm: read, reflect, apply, and encourage one another with concrete next steps.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}The project follows the same biblical commandment foundation presented by Michael Rudolph with Daniel C. Juster in The Law of Messiah. On top of that foundation, Walk as Jesus expands discipleship with practical media collections such as sermons, songs, testimonies, and films to support transformation in heart and action.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}Instead of treating commandments as abstract theory, Walk as Jesus creates a shared learning path where participants challenge and support each other. This strengthens consistency, mutual accountability, and joyful perseverance in obeying Yeshua.{% endblocktrans %}
